sequenceDiagram
    autonumber
    actor Client
    participant PortfolioService
    participant OrdersService
    participant InProgressService
    participant TerminalStateService

    Client->>PortfolioService: Place Order with PlaceOrderRequest
    Note over Client,TerminalStateService: PortfolioService is the front door and owns the account, the cash balance and the holdings, so rules 1 to 7 are its to answer.<br/>OrdersService owns the orders and order_history tables, so rule 8 is its to answer on the unique key.<br/>The eight rules are enforced in this order and the first failure wins.

    %% ------------------------------------------------------------------
    %% RULE 1 - the account must exist - ACC-404
    %% ------------------------------------------------------------------
    PortfolioService->>PortfolioService: look up the account by accountId

    alt RULE 1 FAILS - no account carries that key
        PortfolioService->>OrdersService: record orders row REJECTED and order_history failure_code ACC-404
        PortfolioService-->>Client: ACC-404 Account not found
        Note over PortfolioService,OrdersService: Refused. Rules 2 to 8 are never evaluated.<br/>The attempt is still recorded, because the order table is the audit trail.
    else RULE 1 HOLDS - the account exists

        %% --------------------------------------------------------------
        %% RULE 2 - the account must be ACTIVE - ACC-403
        %% --------------------------------------------------------------
        PortfolioService->>PortfolioService: read the account status

        alt RULE 2 FAILS - the account is SUSPENDED or CLOSED
            PortfolioService->>OrdersService: record orders row REJECTED and order_history failure_code ACC-403
            PortfolioService-->>Client: ACC-403 Account not active
            Note over Client,OrdersService: A suspended account holding no cash stops here with ACC-403 and never reaches rule 6.<br/>The evaluation order is part of the contract, so the first failure wins.<br/>A SUSPENDED account can be read and its suspension is reversible, a CLOSED account never trades again.
        else RULE 2 HOLDS - the account is ACTIVE

            %% ----------------------------------------------------------
            %% RULE 3 - the instrument must exist and be tradable - INS-404
            %% ----------------------------------------------------------
            PortfolioService->>PortfolioService: look up the instrument by symbol and read its tradable flag

            alt RULE 3 FAILS - the symbol is unknown or no longer tradable
                PortfolioService->>OrdersService: record orders row REJECTED and order_history failure_code INS-404
                PortfolioService-->>Client: INS-404 Instrument not found
                Note over PortfolioService,OrdersService: Delisting is a flag and never a deleted row, so a delisted instrument still resolves and is refused here.<br/>Both situations answer INS-404 and only the server log separates them.
            else RULE 3 HOLDS - the instrument exists and is tradable

                %% ------------------------------------------------------
                %% RULE 4 - quantity greater than zero - VAL-422
                %% ------------------------------------------------------
                PortfolioService->>PortfolioService: read quantity off the request

                alt RULE 4 FAILS - quantity is absent, zero or negative
                    PortfolioService->>OrdersService: record orders row REJECTED and order_history failure_code VAL-422
                    PortfolioService-->>Client: VAL-422 Invalid input
                else RULE 4 HOLDS - quantity is greater than zero

                    %% --------------------------------------------------
                    %% RULE 5 - price greater than zero - VAL-422
                    %% --------------------------------------------------
                    PortfolioService->>PortfolioService: read price off the request

                    alt RULE 5 FAILS - price is absent, zero or negative
                        PortfolioService->>OrdersService: record orders row REJECTED and order_history failure_code VAL-422
                        PortfolioService-->>Client: VAL-422 Invalid input
                        Note over Client,OrdersService: Rules 4 and 5 are checked twice on purpose, once as a DTO constraint and once here,<br/>because the domain has to hold for a caller that replays an order and never ran a validator.
                    else RULE 5 HOLDS - price is greater than zero

                        %% ----------------------------------------------
                        %% RULE 6 - a BUY needs the cash - ORD-400
                        %% ----------------------------------------------
                        PortfolioService->>PortfolioService: notional is quantity multiplied by price, then ask the account whether it can afford it, on a BUY only

                        alt RULE 6 FAILS - a BUY whose cost is above the cash balance
                            PortfolioService->>OrdersService: record orders row REJECTED and order_history failure_code ORD-400
                            PortfolioService-->>Client: ORD-400 Insufficient funds
                            Note over Client,OrdersService: The account answers the question and nothing is subtracted to find out,<br/>so a debit that would leave the balance negative is refused before the subtraction.
                        else RULE 6 HOLDS - a SELL, or a BUY the balance covers

                            %% ------------------------------------------
                            %% RULE 7 - a SELL needs the holding - ORD-409
                            %% ------------------------------------------
                            PortfolioService->>PortfolioService: read the held quantity for that symbol, on a SELL only

                            alt RULE 7 FAILS - a SELL larger than the quantity held
                                PortfolioService->>OrdersService: record orders row REJECTED and order_history failure_code ORD-409
                                PortfolioService-->>Client: ORD-409 Insufficient holdings
                                Note over Client,OrdersService: No holding and a holding of zero mean the same thing to rule 7.<br/>A position never goes negative, because short selling is out of scope.
                            else RULE 7 HOLDS - a BUY, or a SELL the holding covers

                                %% --------------------------------------
                                %% RULE 8 - the idempotency key - ORD-409
                                %% --------------------------------------
                                PortfolioService->>OrdersService: create the order with its idempotency_key
                                OrdersService->>OrdersService: claim the key against the unique constraint on orders.idempotency_key
                                Note over OrdersService: A claim and never a read. Two concurrent requests carrying one key both pass a read then write check<br/>and losing that race duplicates a trade, so the write itself is the authority and exactly one caller is told it won.

                                alt RULE 8 FAILS - the key has already been accepted
                                    OrdersService-->>PortfolioService: ORD-409 Duplicate order
                                    PortfolioService-->>Client: ORD-409 Duplicate order
                                    Note over PortfolioService,OrdersService: Nothing is written here. The key belongs to the order that already holds it,<br/>and retrying with the same key is not a way to poll for status.
                                else RULE 8 HOLDS - this request claimed the key

                                    %% ----------------------------------
                                    %% ACCEPTED - all eight rules passed
                                    %% ----------------------------------
                                    OrdersService->>OrdersService: insert orders row status NEW with account_id, instrument_id, side, quantity, price
                                    OrdersService->>OrdersService: insert order_history ORDER_RECEIVED, previous_status none, new_status NEW
                                    OrdersService-->>PortfolioService: order recorded, order_id and status NEW
                                    PortfolioService-->>Client: Order accepted, status NEW
                                    Note over Client,OrdersService: Nothing was mutated until every rule had passed, so a refusal leaves no half changed state.<br/>The order is recorded when it is received, before anyone knows whether it will succeed.

                                    %% ----------------------------------
                                    %% EXECUTION - the order works its way to exactly one terminal state
                                    %% ----------------------------------
                                    OrdersService->>InProgressService: hand over the order sitting in NEW
                                    InProgressService->>OrdersService: insert order_history SUBMITTED_TO_EXTERNAL with request_id
                                    InProgressService->>TerminalStateService: execute the order
                                    TerminalStateService->>TerminalStateService: call the External Trade API and keep the raw response

                                    alt the External Trade API fills it
                                        TerminalStateService->>OrdersService: update orders status FILLED with external_order_id
                                        TerminalStateService->>OrdersService: insert order_history ORDER_FILLED, previous_status NEW, new_status FILLED, api_response kept
                                        TerminalStateService-->>PortfolioService: settle the fill
                                        PortfolioService->>PortfolioService: debit on a BUY or credit on a SELL, and move the holding in the same step
                                        Note over PortfolioService: Cash and position move together or neither moves.<br/>A buy recalculates the average cost, a sell reduces the quantity and leaves the average alone.
                                        PortfolioService-->>Client: Order FILLED, balance and portfolio updated
                                    else the External Trade API refuses it
                                        TerminalStateService->>OrdersService: update orders status REJECTED
                                        TerminalStateService->>OrdersService: insert order_history EXTERNAL_REJECTED with failure_code, failure_reason and api_response
                                        TerminalStateService-->>PortfolioService: nothing to settle
                                        PortfolioService-->>Client: Order REJECTED, no cash and no holding moved
                                    else the customer cancels before a fill
                                        Client->>PortfolioService: Cancel Order
                                        PortfolioService->>InProgressService: stop the order before it fills
                                        InProgressService->>TerminalStateService: cancel the order
                                        TerminalStateService->>OrdersService: update orders status CANCELLED
                                        TerminalStateService->>OrdersService: insert order_history ORDER_CANCELLED, previous_status NEW, new_status CANCELLED
                                        PortfolioService-->>Client: Order CANCELLED
                                    end
                                    Note over Client,TerminalStateService: NEW is the working state and FILLED, REJECTED and CANCELLED are terminal.<br/>An order reaches exactly one of them and a disallowed transition is refused rather than applied.<br/>There is no partial fill literal, so the External Trade API fills in full or refuses.
                                end
                            end
                        end
                    end
                end
            end
        end
    end
